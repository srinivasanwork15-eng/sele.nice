01. About Me

Crafting code with intention and curiosity

A glimpse into who I am, why I build for the web, and how I work.

Hello! I'm Srinivasan. My journey into web development started when I realized that every digital product I love began as lines of code in a simple text editor. That sparked a deep passion for understanding how pixels, logic, and human interactions come together.

As a junior developer, I spend every day building real-world projects, studying semantic markup, mastering CSS layouts (Flexbox and Grid), and deepening my grasp of core JavaScript fundamentals. Rather than rushing into complex frameworks, I prioritized learning the web platform from the ground up.

When I'm not coding, you can find me reviewing accessibility guidelines, participating in open-source discussions, reading technical articles, or experimenting with micro-interactions.
